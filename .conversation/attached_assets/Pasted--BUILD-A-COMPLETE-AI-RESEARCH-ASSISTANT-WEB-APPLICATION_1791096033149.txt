# BUILD A COMPLETE AI RESEARCH ASSISTANT WEB APPLICATION

You are an expert full-stack developer, UI/UX designer, AI engineer, and multi-agent systems architect.

Build a complete, polished, production-style web application called:

# Research Rescue AI 🔬

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Research Rescue AI is an AI-powered research assistant designed specifically for students.

The purpose of the application is to help students research any academic topic without manually searching Google, opening many websites, reading large amounts of information, comparing sources, and creating notes themselves.

The student enters a research question such as:

"What causes antibiotic resistance?"

The system automatically performs the following workflow:

Student Question
↓
Research Planner
↓
Research Finder
↓
Research Analyst
↓
Final Research Report

The application must use three specialized AI agents:

### Agent 1 — Research Planner

Understands the student's research question and creates a research plan.

It should determine:

* What the question is asking
* Main concepts that need investigation
* Important sub-questions
* Keywords/search queries
* Types of sources required
* Research scope
* What information should be compared

Example:

Student question:
"What causes antibiotic resistance?"

Research Planner should generate:

Research objective:
Understand the biological, medical, and human factors that contribute to antibiotic resistance.

Research areas:

1. Overuse of antibiotics
2. Incorrect antibiotic use
3. Bacterial mutations
4. Horizontal gene transfer
5. Agricultural antibiotic use
6. Poor infection control
7. Lack of new antibiotics

Search queries:

* causes of antibiotic resistance
* antibiotic resistance mechanisms
* antibiotic overuse and resistance
* bacterial horizontal gene transfer
* antibiotic resistance WHO

Do not simply repeat the student's question. Break it into useful research tasks.

---

### Agent 2 — Research Finder

The Research Finder searches the internet or available search/web tools for reliable information based on the Research Planner's research plan.

Prioritize reliable sources such as:

* Government organizations
* Universities
* Peer-reviewed research
* Scientific organizations
* Official organizations
* Reputable educational institutions
* Major research databases
* High-quality scientific publications

Examples:

* WHO
* CDC
* NIH
* Universities
* Government health departments
* Peer-reviewed journals

Avoid relying heavily on:

* Random blogs
* Unverified websites
* SEO spam
* Content farms
* Social media posts
* Sources without authorship or credibility

For every source collect:

* Source title
* Website/organization
* URL
* Publication date if available
* Author if available
* Relevant information
* Why the source is useful
* Source credibility
* Research topic/category

The Research Finder should attempt to find multiple independent sources rather than depending on one website.

Aim for approximately 5–10 useful sources depending on the research question.

If web search is unavailable, clearly communicate that limitation instead of pretending that live research was performed.

---

### Agent 3 — Research Analyst

The Research Analyst receives the research plan and collected sources.

Its job is to:

1. Read the collected information.
2. Extract important facts.
3. Identify common findings.
4. Identify differences between sources.
5. Detect conflicting claims.
6. Remove irrelevant information.
7. Rank information by importance.
8. Avoid unsupported claims.
9. Create a simple student-friendly research report.
10. Include citations/references for claims.

The analyst must NEVER invent sources, URLs, statistics, studies, quotations, or facts.

If information cannot be verified, clearly label it as uncertain.
